This paper proposes a two-stage weakly supervised fine-grained aircraft detection framework that eliminates reliance on large-scale fine-grained annotations. It introduces a Prior-knowledge-based Soft-label Generator (PSG) to propagate fine-grained labels, a Text Prototype Alignment (TPA) strategy to enhance semantic discrimination, and a Feature Purification Module (FPM) to suppress background noise, achieving significant performance gains with only 1% labeled data.
